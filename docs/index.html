About LTI Property Advisors

LTI Property Advisors is a Houston real estate company focused on helping property owners with off-market property solutions. We work with homeowners who want a fast sale, a simple process, or help evaluating a property opportunity.


We buy properties directly — no agents, no listings, no open houses. If your property fits our criteria, we'll make you a straightforward cash offer and work on a timeline that works for you.
