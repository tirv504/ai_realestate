Privacy Policy

LTI Property Advisors  |  Effective Date: May 24, 2026

Summary: We collect your name, phone number, and property address when you request a cash offer. We use it only to contact you about your inquiry. We do not sell, share, or rent your information to third parties or affiliates.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit our property inquiry form, we collect the following information you voluntarily provide:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Property address
  • Any additional details you choose to share

We do not collect payment information, Social Security numbers, or sensitive personal data through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Contact you regarding your property inquiry and cash offer request
  • Send SMS and/or voice messages related to your submission, if you have provided consent
  • Respond to your questions and follow-up requests
  • Improve our services and communication processes

We do not use your information for automated profiling, targeted advertising, or any purpose unrelated to your property inquiry.

3. SMS and Phone Communications

By checking the consent boxes on our inquiry form, you expressly agree to receive SMS text messages and/or phone calls from LTI Property Advisors at the phone number you provided. These messages may include:

  • Promotional and marketing messages about cash offer opportunities
  • Transactional messages such as offer updates, appointment reminders, and follow-up communications

Message frequency varies based on the status of your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan.

To opt out of SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message we send. You will receive one confirmation message and no further SMS contact. To request assistance, reply HELP or contact us at info@ltipropertyadvisors.com.

Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of any purchase or service.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information — including your name and phone number — with third parties or affiliates for any marketing or promotional purpose.

We may share your information only with trusted service providers (such as form processing platforms and communication tools) that assist us in delivering our services. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality obligations and are prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing services to us.

No personal data is shared with unaffiliated third parties.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. If you request deletion of your data, we will honor that request within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for SMS communications at any time by replying STOP

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@ltipropertyadvisors.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
