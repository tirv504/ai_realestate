We Help Homeowners Move Forward

LTI Property Advisors is a Houston-based real estate advisory firm focused on giving homeowners a fast, fair path to selling their property — without the hassle of the traditional market.

Who We Are

A Different Kind of Real Estate Firm

LTI Property Advisors was built around one simple idea: selling your home should not be stressful. We work directly with homeowners across the Houston metro area who need to sell on their timeline — not the market's.

We are not a listing brokerage. We are a private acquisition firm that buys properties directly. That means no open houses, no waiting on buyer financing, no last-minute deals falling through. We make an offer, you decide, and we close on your schedule.

What We Do

Direct Cash Offers on Houston Properties

We evaluate properties throughout Harris County and the broader Houston MSA. Once a homeowner reaches out, our team reviews the property, reaches out to learn about the situation and timeline, and provides a no-obligation cash offer.

There are no commissions, no repair requirements, and no obligations of any kind. If our offer works for you, we move forward. If it doesn't, there's no pressure and no hard feelings.
